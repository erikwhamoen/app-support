Table for Four

Support & Privacy Policy

About the app

Table for Four (Kaart Tafel in Dutch) is a free, ad-free collection of six classic card games — Hearts, Oh Hell, Rikken, Toepen, Crazy Eights, and Spades — played against three computer opponents. No account, no ads, no in-app purchases, and no internet connection needed. Developed by Erik Hamoen.

Privacy Policy

Data we collect: none. Table for Four does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, and it never connects to the internet.

  • No account or sign-in of any kind
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising
  • No third-party SDKs or services
  • No network access — the app works fully offline; nothing is ever sent from your device

Local settings. Your chosen difficulty level and language preference are stored locally on your device using standard iOS app preferences. This information stays on your device — it is never transmitted anywhere and never leaves your control.
