Petra & Erik Paris

Support & Privacy Policy

About the app

Petra & Erik Paris is a personal guide to Paris by Petra and Erik — two Amsterdammers with an apartment in le Marais. The app contains their curated list of favourite places: restaurants, bars, shops, cultural venues, and outdoor tips. No ads, no accounts, no nonsense.

Privacy Policy

Data we collect: none. The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

  • No account or registration required
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data

Content updates. The app fetches a content file from a public URL on GitHub to keep recommendations up to date. No personal data is included in this request — it is a plain download with no identifying information. The content is cached on your device for offline use.

Location. The app asks for access to your location to show your position on the map. Your location is used only on your device and is never sent to us or to any third party. You can use the app without granting location access — you will simply not see your own position on the map.
